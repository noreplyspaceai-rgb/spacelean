# SpaceLean Pricing Tiers

## Free Tier
- Price: $0/month
- Limits: 1 active project, basic AI task generator, 4D BIM viewer up to 50MB
- Target: Individual engineers and small sub-teams

## Pro Tier
- Price: $19/user/month (billed annually) | $25/user/month (billed monthly)
- Limits: Unlimited projects, 1,000-simulation predictive risk engine, Primavera P6 XML export
- Target: General Contractors and VDC managers

## Enterprise Tier
- Price: Custom contract
- Limits: Unlimited projects & storage, dedicated onboarding, custom BIM API integrations, SSO, SLA
- Target: Enterprise GCs, Engineering Firms, and Real Estate Developers
